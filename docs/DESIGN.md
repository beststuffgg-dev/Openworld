# Design Vision

The north star for Project Horizons, preserved from the original brief so the
roadmap has something to aim at. Nothing here is a promise about the current
build — see `ROADMAP.md` for what actually exists. This is the destination.

## One-line pitch

A polished, modern evolution of Minecraft: the freedom of voxel building inside a
living, procedurally generated world with deep RPG systems, engaging multiplayer,
and soft, high-quality visuals — scaling from mid-range phones to gaming PCs.

## Pillars

1. **Handcrafted feel from procedural systems.** Terrain, biomes, structures and
   villages are generated, but tuned and templated so the world reads as
   authored, not random. Soft biome borders, erosion and structure placement all
   serve this.
2. **Less blocky, still readable.** Cubic voxels, but softened with ambient
   occlusion, bevelled edges, PBR materials, connected textures and blended
   foliage. Readability of gameplay always wins over visual spectacle.
3. **A world that lives without the player.** Day/night drives NPC schedules;
   weather and seasons change the land, crops and animals; villages work, defend
   and remember the player.
4. **Freedom + consequence.** Build anything; survive hunger, cold and enemies;
   pursue a main story about a world-ending threat, or ignore it and homestead.
5. **One game, every screen.** The same experience on Windows, Android and iOS,
   with input and performance that adapt to the device.

## Target visual language

"Minecraft with Bliss-style shaders": dynamic global illumination, volumetric
clouds and fog, god rays, bloom, SSR/SSGI, dynamic shadows, animated wind-driven
grass and leaves, water caustics and realistic water, weather effects (rain
splashes, snow accumulation, wet terrain, puddles), and a rich sky — sunsets,
auroras, stars, the Milky Way, moon phases and lightning.

## Systems at a glance

World generation · voxel building · survival · crafting · farming · combat ·
animals & taming · NPC villages · quests & story · progression & skills ·
weather & seasons · multiplayer with guilds/economy · creative mode · deep
settings & accessibility · mobile optimisation.

The full enumerated feature list from the brief is intentionally captured in
`ROADMAP.md`, where each feature is tied to a phase and a status, so the vision
stays honest about what is built versus what is planned.
